# Ordinary makings

> *The shelf is older than the road.*
> *What is spoken is the true name; what is worn is the ruin.*

**Ordinary makings** is an open, growing worldbuilding project — a registry of characters called **houses** (or **Epithets**), each built from a biological lineage, a vow, a poem, and a name. It began as a quartet of ecology-obsessed characters and grew, law by law, into a census of **twenty-five seats**.

Sixteen are filed. Nine remain open. The count is accurate.

---

## The method

Every house is built the same way, in the same order:

1. **Clade.** A house holds a lineage — a radiate grade, a eukaryotic supergroup, a prokaryotic kingdom, a viral realm. The clade is the territory.
2. **Ethos.** Not an archetype — a **vow**. Each house is an ecological question: *What can wait? How many should there be? What turns the end into fuel?* The vow is the seed; everything else grows from it.
3. **Poem.** The poem is the constitution. **Every line rests on a verifiable record** — a fact ledger travels with every poem. The poems read as family letters; they are also seminars in microbiology, virology, etymology, and history.
4. **Name.** Names migrate (Iullus → Libo → Caesar is the Roman precedent; most houses migrated two to four times before arriving). The formal name is paperwork. **The kitchen name is the true name.**

---

## The laws

The registry discovered its laws by collision, not decree. They govern everything filed after them:

1. **Niche law** — one Epithet, one territory. No overlap, linguistic or biological. (One house died of this law: a Greek pea-star name, competitively excluded, later adopted into the plant house's bloodline. The registry keeps its dead.)
2. **Ethos law** — no archetypes; the vow is the seed; the poem is the constitution.
3. **Stem law** — the road from LUCA to *Homo sapiens* belongs to the Human. Houses sit only at the **exits** — the forks the road passed by. *Passenger clause:* a side branch may board the road; engine, never driver.
4. **Meta-law (the Two Shelves)** — the creator's archive is one body split, like Francia. Where the archive splits, the names cross. The registry does not believe in coincidence. It believes in the aisle.

**The rank note:** the viral houses own entire **realms** — the viral equivalent of a domain. No cellular house owns a domain. The smallest bodies on the shelf wear the grandest rank, and at that rank the virosphere is officially trait-based, not descent-based: the realms are ethos-houses by law.

## How a house document is built

Each house keeps an **Original** document, in this order:

1. **The poem** — the constitution, signed by the clade.
2. **The roster** — formal name, kitchen name, tongue, clade, ethos, house question.
3. **The Name** — the name's migrations and layers; every rejected draft is kept. (The Don's surname migrated four times; the kitchen name never moved.)
4. **The Records Under The Poem** — the fact ledger; every stanza's stone.
5. **The Birth Concept** — the house's family idea (the Sleepers, the Riders, the Overprints, the Folds…), pending the creator's ruling.
6. **The Houses, Noted** — pairings, rivalries, debts, and grudges with the other houses.
7. **Open slots** — deliberate, do not close. (Adnaviria's name stays open until a speaker of !Xóõ verifies a compound. The kingdom stays unnamed. The stone does not say which stone it is.)

## Pairings, for the curious

- **The Two Doors** — Don Magno (West) and Khayira (East). The meaning demolished vs. the structure demolished. *West: the form stands, the meaning migrates. East: the structure migrates, the beloved stands.* Every crossing chooses: pay the fee, or be entered.
- **The Exchanged Pair** — Quinn and Lulu-Gégé. The German house wears a French-origin armor (*Panzer* ← Old French *pancier*, the belly-mail); the French house wears a Frankish spear (*Gérald* ← *ger* + *wald*). The Franks split into France and Germany; the names crossed at the split.
- **The eldest pair** — Imdua (the first house) and Adnaviria (the eldest rider, possibly at LUCA). The stone keeps a record of every wave that ever got in.

## Colophon

Built in long conversation between the creator and an agentic assistant. The poems, names, and vows are the creator's. The records are verified against the scientific and historical literature, and the verifications travel with the poems. Some doors are left ajar on purpose; some questions (*mere sublimate, or requiem?*) are not closed.

The registry waits. Waiting is a house law anyway.
